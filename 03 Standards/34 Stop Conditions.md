# 34 Stop Conditions

Long-horizon research fails in two directions: stopping before the load-bearing questions are answered, and continuing long after new material stopped changing anything. Both are avoidable by naming the condition in advance.

## Stop when one of these fires

1. **Resolution.** Every ★ claim in the ledger is `supported`, `refuted`, or explicitly `unknown` at the target confidence from the brief.
2. **Saturation.** The last several evidence entries changed no ledger status. New sources are repeating known material. This is a real and sufficient reason to stop — and it is the one most often ignored.
3. **Irreducibility.** The remaining uncertainty cannot be resolved by any available source. The information is not public, not yet produced, or genuinely contested among people who would know. Record what would resolve it and stop.
4. **Budget.** The depth set at intake is spent. Deliver what exists with the gaps stated, rather than delivering late and complete.

Record which condition fired, in `05 Decision Log.md`. One line.

## Do not stop because

- A lot of material has been gathered. Volume is not resolution.
- The conclusion feels satisfying. That feeling arrives well before the evidence justifies it, and it arrives *earlier* when the conclusion matches the opening prior.
- A ★ question is hard. Hard and load-bearing means it gets more effort, not a downgrade to context.
- The deliverable is already long enough.

## Do not continue because

- More could theoretically be found. It always could.
- One unstarred question remains open. Context questions do not gate delivery.
- The answer is uncomfortable and more searching might change it. Check the evidence log: if disconfirming searches were run and logged, the discomfort is information, not a gap.

## Partial delivery beats late delivery

A dated answer with explicit gaps, delivered when it is needed, is worth more than a complete one that arrives after the decision. If the budget is spent with ★ questions open:

- Deliver the resolved parts with the open ones named
- Put the open ones at the top of `04 Open Threads.md`
- Say in the deliverable what a further block of work would buy

## Sunk cost

Effort already spent is not a reason to continue, and it is not a reason to inflate the conclusion. A two-week project that ends in "this cannot be determined from public sources, and here is why" is a successful project, and stating it plainly is the whole value.
