# 15 Deliverable

The only polished artifact in the project. Everything upstream stays rough on purpose; the effort saved there is spent here.

## Pick the form by use, not by habit

| The reader will | Build |
|---|---|
| Read it once and decide | Memo — markdown, or `.docx` via the **docx** skill |
| Change inputs and re-run it | Spreadsheet — **xlsx** skill |
| Present it to others | Deck — a Slides artifact, or **pptx** skill if a file is needed |
| Check back on it repeatedly | A published Artifact — dashboard or tracker |
| Extend it into a paper | Outline first, then prose — see `02 Domains/22 Academic Paper.md` |

Ambiguous cases default to a memo. A memo can become anything; a deck cannot become a memo.

Charts follow the **dataviz** skill. For which skill builds what, and why output-format skills are only opened at this stage: `05 Meta/51 Skills and capabilities.md`.

## Structure

1. **The answer** — first, in under 100 words, with its confidence. Not a summary of the process.
2. **The case** — the chain from `13 Synthesis`, with sources inline.
3. **What would change it** — the strongest opposing case from `14 Adversarial Review`, stated fairly and briefly.
4. **What was not established** — the unknown bucket, and what was searched for and not found.
5. **As-of** — the date, and the vintage of the most stale load-bearing figure.

Writing conventions: `03 Standards/33 Writing Standards.md`.

## Traceability

Every material claim traces to an evidence ID or is marked as inference. Every number carries a source and a date. If a claim cannot be traced, it does not go in — a deliverable is a claim about what is *known*, and an untraceable sentence is a claim about what is *felt*.

## Close the record

The project is not finished when the deliverable is sent. Before finishing:

1. `04 Open Threads.md` — append what remains open and, at the top, the stage to rejoin if work resumes
2. `05 Decision Log.md` — append the final position and the stop condition that fired (`03 Standards/34 Stop Conditions.md`)
3. `00 Brief.md` — record the deliverable's path and date under a **Delivered** heading

This takes two minutes and is what makes the project resumable in six weeks. Skipping it is how a research folder becomes an archive nobody can re-enter.

## Delivering the file

Deliverables are written into the project folder, and — when a folder is connected — also delivered into the conversation so they can be opened directly. Drafts are worth sending as they are produced, not held to the end.
